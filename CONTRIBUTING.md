# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in DLsite Client.

## Pull requests are not accepted

**Do not open pull requests.** External PRs will be closed without merge.

## How to contribute

1. Open a **GitHub Issue** describing the bug, idea, or doc gap.
2. Include enough detail to reproduce (OS, steps, logs under the configured log folder) when reporting bugs.
3. Discussion and any follow-up changes happen through issues; the maintainers apply fixes on their own branches.
